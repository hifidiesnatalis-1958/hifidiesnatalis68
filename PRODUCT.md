# Dies Natalis HIFI ke-68
## Register
Brand campaign with member interactions and a separate product-style committee panel.
## Platform
Web. Responsive JSX application deployed to Vercel, Google Apps Script and Sheets backend.
## Purpose
Information and participation for Dies Natalis HIFI FMIPA UNPAD, 2026, theme Kasundaan.
## Confirmed scope
Menfess, raffle, schedule, event logo and theme explanation, dress code, messages and impressions, event location/map.
Users choose their own unique nickname and password to register and log in, following the KKN site's server-side password hashes and cookie sessions. Nicknames support spaces, symbols and Unicode, preserving display capitalization. No Google OAuth or email is required. Public registration always creates a regular account; committee accounts are provisioned separately with a server-only command. Menfess is always anonymous, including in the committee panel. Committee operators can present approved, screened messages in shuffled order with fullscreen and autoplay controls. Raffle signup requires a logged-in account; one account receives one ticket.
## Content constraints
Online activities 17 October; main celebration 24 October 2026. Location, dress code, and additional schedule follow later. Never present guessed details as official.
